# Business-for-sale directory

A directory of operating businesses for sale in Indonesia. It helps buyers contact owners; it does not broker or complete sales.

## Language

**Business**:
A whole operating business offered for sale. Minority stakes, asset-only sales, property-only sales, and offers to open a new franchise are outside this scope.

**Listing**:
The description of a business offered for sale, including its asking price, location, photos, and authorized WhatsApp contact.

**Owner**:
The seller of the business who supplies listing details and permits their public use. An owner is not a broker acting for another seller.

**Buyer**:
A person who browses businesses for sale and can contact an owner through WhatsApp.

**Asking price**:
The owner's public price for the business in IDR. It is not a platform valuation.
_Avoid_: Valuation

**Published listing**:
A listing available to buyers. It contains only approved public content.

**Listing approval**:
An admin's decision that a listing is ready for public display. It does not verify financial claims, certify ownership, or guarantee a sale.
_Avoid_: Verification, certification

**Authorized WhatsApp contact**:
The exact WhatsApp number approved for public exposure as the listing's owner contact. It can be a personal or business number.

**Owner-reported financial figures**:
Optional revenue or profit figures supplied by the owner, with their period and meaning stated. The directory has not verified them.

**Delivered enquiry**:
A buyer enquiry that reaches the owner's contact. A WhatsApp click alone does not establish delivery, and this pilot does not measure it.
